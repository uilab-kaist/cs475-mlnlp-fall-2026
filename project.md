## Goal

The goal of this project is to critically analyze an existing paper in NLP or machine learning and develop a meaningful extension based on that analysis.

Rather than simply reproducing an existing paper, you should identify an interesting research question, limitation, or opportunity for improvement and investigate it through well-motivated experiments.

Possible directions include, but are not limited to:

- Extending or modifying the research question
- Improving or modifying the model or method
- Applying the approach to a new task, domain, or dataset
- Investigating limitations or failure cases
- Introducing new features or data
- Improving the experimental design or evaluation methodology
- Re-evaluating the conclusions of the original work under different conditions

The emphasis of the project is on **research reasoning, experimental design, analysis, and interpretation**, rather than simply implementing an existing method.

## Instructions

### Paper Selection

Choose a paper that will serve as the **starting point or baseline paper** for your project.

- Please refer to the paper list (list TBD) and select a paper. Papers from the list will be assigned on a **first-come-first-served basis**.
- Alternatively, you may choose your own paper. The paper should generally satisfy the following criteria:
  - A recent paper published in **2024 or later**
  - A technical paper that includes a machine learning model and a medium- to large-sized dataset
  - An NLP or machine learning paper published at **ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, TACL, COLM, NeurIPS, ICML, or ICLR**

If your selected paper deviates substantially from these criteria, or if your project is not based on an existing paper, please email the teaching staff in advance.


### Project Direction

Based on the selected paper, you may pursue one of the following directions:

1. **Apply and investigate**: Apply the method or idea from the baseline paper to a new research problem, task, domain, or dataset, and investigate how well it generalizes.

2. **Analyze and improve**: Identify a limitation, failure case, or open question in the baseline paper and propose an extension or modification to address it.

Your project should go beyond simply running the existing code or reproducing reported numbers. You should formulate a clear research question and conduct experiments that provide meaningful evidence for answering it.

## Project Proposal

Give a **5–8 minute proposal presentation** that includes the following:

- **Problem**:
  - What problem are you studying?
  - What is the specific task?
  - Why is this problem important?

- **Related Work**:
  - What existing papers have studied this problem?
  - What approaches have been proposed?

- **Baseline Paper**:
  - Which paper will use for your project?
  - What are its main research questions, methods, and findings?
  - Why did you choose this paper?

- **Project Direction**:
  - What limitation, open question, or opportunity for extension have you identified?
  - What research question or hypothesis will your project investigate?
  - How will your project extend, modify, or re-evaluate the baseline work?

- **Data**:
  - Describe the dataset(s) you plan to use.
  - If the baseline paper does not use openly available data, explain how you will obtain or construct the necessary data.
  - If you use only a subset of an existing dataset, explain why and how the subset will be selected.

- **Evaluation Plan**:
  - What experiments will you conduct?
  - What baselines, metrics, or comparisons will you use to evaluate your research question?

- **Plan**:
  - Provide a week-by-week plan for completing the project.
  - Clearly indicate which tasks will be done by each team member and which tasks will be done together.


## Progress Presentation

Upload a **7–10 minute progress presentation video** that includes the following:

- **Introduction**:
  - Briefly explain your problem, approach, and dataset.

- **Baseline Paper Analysis and Preliminary Findings**:
  - Describe the baseline methods or experiments you have implemented or evaluated so far, including the experimental setup and preliminary results.
  - Analyze what you learned from these results, including any limitations, unexpected findings, or opportunities for improvement.
  - If some experiments are still in progress, present the results you have so far and a concrete plan for completing the remaining work.

- **Project Direction**:
  - Based on your preliminary findings, explain how you plan to extend, improve, or modify the original work.
  - Clearly motivate why your proposed direction is meaningful and what hypothesis or research question you plan to investigate.
  - Describe the concrete experiments you will conduct for the final project.


## Final Presentation & Final Report

Give a **10–15 minute final presentation** and submit a **4–6 page report (+ unlimited references)** in ACL format.

The final presentation should cover the same core content as the report, presented in an appropriate presentation format.

The final report should include the following:

- **Abstract**:
  - Summary of your research question, approach, and main findings

- **Introduction**:
  - Background, motivation, and research question

- **Related Work**:
  - Relevant prior work and the relationship between your project and the baseline paper

- **Approach**:
  - Your proposed approach, modification, extension, or analysis

- **Data & Experiments**:
  - Dataset(s), models, experimental setup, evaluation metrics, and comparison methods

- **Results**:
  - Results from the baseline methods or experiments
  - Results from your proposed approach or investigation
  - Appropriate tables, figures, and quantitative or qualitative analyses
  - Interpretation of the results in relation to your research question

- **Discussion**:
  - What did you learn from the experiments?
  - How do your findings extend, challenge, or complement the baseline paper?
  - What are the limitations of your project?

- **Conclusion**:
  - Summary of the main findings and implications

- **References**:
  - Unlimited pages
